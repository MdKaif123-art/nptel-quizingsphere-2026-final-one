The Quizingsphere Journey

A personal message from Mohammed Kaif K | Creator & Admin

🌱

Semester 3: The Humble Beginning

What started out as a small personal project in my 3rd semester—built simply to help myself study and make practicing NPTEL questions easier for my peers—began touching lives in ways I never imagined.

🚀

5 Semesters & 25,000+ Students Strong

Over the past 5 semesters, Quizingsphere grew into an incredible community of over 25,000+ learners across India. Balancing hectic university schedules, exams, and personal workloads, I made it my personal priority to keep every single question accurate, reliable, and close to 100% consistent every single week.

❤️

Built by Community, Shaped by Feedback

I’ve received all kinds of feedback along the way—some loved the UI, some offered constructive critiques, but every word helped shape this platform. This wouldn’t have been possible without everyone who shared questions, contributed information, and supported each other with genuine kindness.

🌅

The Final Semester Serving You

Quizingsphere has been supporting students across 5 semesters, and this is going to be the last semester I will be helping out and actively guiding NPTEL exams. After this, the platform will be taking a well-deserved rest for some time. To everyone whose messages or DMs I couldn’t get back to—I had a lot going on behind the scenes, and I hope you understand. I’m truly proud of the community we built together.
