Week 1: Assignment 1

⚡ Practice as Quiz
1. What is mental well-being?
  • Absence of mental illness
  • Emotional, psychological, and social well-being, affecting how we think, feel, and act
  • A state of complete happiness at all times
  • Having a high IQ and academic success
Accepted Answer: Emotional, psychological, and social well-being, affecting how we think, feel, and act
2. Which of the following is a common emotional change during adolescence?
  • Constant mental stability
  • Decreased sensitivity to peer influence
  • Increased emotional fluctuations
  • Decline in identity exploration
Accepted Answer: Increased emotional fluctuations
3. Why is identity formation significant during adolescence?
  • It helps adolescents avoid peer interactions
  • It promotes dependency on parental opinions
  • It shapes their sense of self and direction in life
  • It increases academic pressure
Accepted Answer: It shapes their sense of self and direction in life
4. Which of the following is an example of emotional intelligence?
  • Ignoring other people's feelings
  • Understanding and managing one's own emotions
  • Memorizing emotional words
  • Always agreeing with friends
Accepted Answer: Understanding and managing one's own emotions
5. Why are support networks important in times of crisis?
  • They isolate individuals from emotional impact
  • They help with financial planning only
  • They provide emotional and psychological safety
  • They reduce the need for communication
Accepted Answer: They provide emotional and psychological safety
6. What role do mentors and guidance play in adolescents' well-being?
  • Discourage independence
  • Promote critical thinking and offer life advice
  • Promote critical thinking and offer life advice
  • Enforce academic pressure
  • Limit emotional growth
Accepted Answer: Promote critical thinking and offer life advice
7. Which of the following is a practical stress management technique?
  • Overthinking
  • Avoiding problems
  • Mindfulness and relaxation
  • Ignoring support systems
Accepted Answer: Mindfulness and relaxation
8. According to Erikson, what is the central conflict during adolescence?
  • Trust vs. Mistrust
  • Initiative vs. Guilt
  • Identity vs. Role Confusion
  • Autonomy vs. Shame and Doubt
Accepted Answer: Identity vs. Role Confusion
9. According to Kristin Neff, which of the following is not one of the three core components of self-compassion?
  • Self-criticism
  • Common Humanity
  • Mindfulness
  • Self-kindness
Accepted Answer: Self-criticism
10. What are the two main types of social comparison identified in Festinger's Social Comparison Theory?
  • Objective and Subjective Comparison
  • Internal and External Comparison
  • Upward and Downward Comparison
  • Realistic and Unrealistic Comparison
Accepted Answer: Upward and Downward Comparison
